## Brand Story

## The Legend We Tell

Stan Acton has been a builder since he was ten.

The day he broke his father’s heirloom mallet, he grabbed a piece of firewood, climbed onto his
grandfather’s

Shopsmith

lathe,

and

turned

a

new

one.
Work the problem. Respect the craft.
Make

it

right.
That instinct never left him.
He put himself through college as an apprentice to John Warren, a remodeling contractor who
taught

him

two

lessons

that

still

define

this

company:
quality is non-negotiable, and integrity
is

everything. After four years in Silicon Valley tech, the craft won. In 1990, Stan founded Acton
Construction

—

and

spent

thirty

years

earning

a

reputation

across

the

Bay

Area

for

design-build

work

that

was

functional,

lasting,

and

charming.

Delivered

with

certainty,

transparency,

and

value.
No surprises. Ever.
Then in 2017, standing in his Naglee Park closet under a swinging light bulb, Stan saw what
was

coming

for

California.

Housing costs climbing. Families getting priced out of the neighborhoods they’d lived in for
generations.

Aging

parents

needing

care

close

by.

Adult

children

needing

a

place

to

land.

Homeowners

needing

a

way

to

expand

their

property’s

value

—

and

their

family’s

future

—

without

leaving

home.

He didn’t just see a building problem. He saw a family problem.
So he named it, and built a movement around solving it: Family Integrity Planning — a way for
California

homeowners

to

keep

generations

close,

care

for

the

people

they

love,

and

grow

lasting

value

in

the

place

they

already

call

home.

ADUs are how we make it real.
Today, we’re Acton ADU. We build premium ADUs that homeowners are proud to call home —
and

proud

to

have

built.

We

navigate

the

complexity.

We

protect

the

budget,

the

schedule,

and

the

trust.

And

we

design

every

project

to

grow

in

value

for

the

generations

who

will

live

in

it.

Stan saw the problem from a swinging light bulb in a closet.
We’re solving it, one backyard at a time.

## Thematic Supports

## Three Categories. Nine Specific Commitments.

Every one is something a homeowner can hold us accountable for.

PROCESS The structure that makes premium delivery possible. Structure — We follow a proven sequence so homeowners always know what’s next.
Education — We teach as we go, so homeowners make informed decisions with
confidence.

Expectations — We set expectations early and revisit them at every milestone.
RESPECT The way we honor the homeowner’s time, money, and trust. Budget — We design within the homeowner’s budget, not around it.
Schedule — We commit to milestones up front and protect them through every phase.
Communication — We over-communicate by default — silence is a service failure.
DELIVERY The outcomes that make Acton premium.

Guaranteed Results — We stand behind every build with a warranty we honor without
friction.

Premium Service — We deliver Process and Respect at a level the industry doesn’t match.
Options — We design ADUs that adapt to family, guests, or income — sometimes all three
over

time.

## The Foundation

## No Surprises. Thoughtful Procedures. Pride And Quality.

Every layer of the brand rests on the same foundation. Every team member, in every role, can
read

this

and

ask

one

question

every

day:

Am I delivering No Surprises, following Thoughtful Procedures, and
producing

Pride

and

Quality?
NO SURPRISES What it means. The homeowner always knows what’s next, what it costs, and what to expect. The job. Set expectations early. Communicate proactively. Surface issues before they grow.
THOUGHTFUL PROCEDURES What it means. Every step is intentional, sequenced, and built on 35 years of hard-earned
judgment.
The job. Follow the process. Improve the process. Never skip the process.
PRIDE AND QUALITY What it means. The work earns pride from the homeowner, the family, and the craftsmen who
built

it.
The job. Hold the line on quality. Sweat the details. Refuse shortcuts.

That’s how the brand stops being words on a page — and becomes the way the work gets
done.
