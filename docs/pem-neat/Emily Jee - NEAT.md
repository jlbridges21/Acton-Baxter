# **SECTION 1 — SALES INTELLIGENCE**

## **1\. Customer Story**

Emily and Brian are newly married later in life and are trying to merge households without forcing everyone into an uncomfortable living arrangement. Emily owns the home, Brian currently rents nearby, and the garage conversion ADU would give Brian his own private living space while keeping them close. The space also supports Emily’s daughter when visiting from college/medical school, family guests, and potential caregiver flexibility as they age.

## **2\. Customer Pain**

The core pain is the tension between wanting to live life together as a married couple while still preserving independence, routines, comfort, and family stability. The main house is already full, with Emily’s daughter and sister occupying bedrooms, so Brian moving fully into the home is not practical right now. They are trying to avoid disrupting the household while also reducing the inconvenience and cost of Brian continuing to rent separately.

## **3\. Type 1 Pain — Why Build an ADU**

- Create a private living space for Brian on Emily’s property.
- Support a later-in-life blended household without forcing everyone into the main house.
- Provide flexible future use for guests, Emily’s daughter, family, or a caregiver.
- Potentially reduce Brian’s ongoing rent expense.

## **4\. Type 2 Pain — Why Choose Acton**

Emily and Brian care heavily about communication, transparency, and avoiding surprises. Emily specifically described success as good communication, clear handling of unexpected issues, agreement on resolutions, and clarity on budget/timeline impact. They are detail-oriented and want a professional partner who can evaluate the garage properly, explain infrastructure costs, and manage risk before they commit deeply.

No hard decision deadline was set. Ideal completion would be before Emily’s daughter potentially leaves for school in 2027, but they are not rushing. Decision makers appear to be Emily and Brian together.

## **7\. Acton Recommendation**
