# User guides

## CEO and COO

You are the only people who can approve Sam's actions. Either of you may approve.

**Approving**

- Open **Sam Operations → Approvals** (the badge shows how many are waiting), or the general **Approvals** page.
- Each request shows what Sam wants to do, the recipient or record, the evidence, and the expected effect.
- Select **Show exact details being approved** to see the precise content. Your approval covers exactly that content.
- **Approve** runs it. **Decline** stops it, and the step shows that it was declined.
- To change something, decline it and ask for a revised request. Approvals can't be edited in place.
- Nothing happens if you don't respond. Overdue items are escalated to you; they are never approved automatically.

**Asking Sam**

- In the AI Workspace, or in Slack once your Slack account is linked, address Sam by name. For example:
  - "Sam, what's waiting for my approval?"
  - "Sam, which contracts expire in the next 90 days?"
  - "Sam, start onboarding for Jordan, starting March 3 — offer accepted today."
- Sam answers from the registers and can **request** a workflow start or queue a message. Each of those still comes to you for approval.

**Your other controls**

- Setup (leadership decisions, HR administrators, finance owner, legal entities).
- Autonomy (enable or revoke).
- Skip a step with a recorded reason.
- Cancel a workflow.
- The COO alone completes **access** steps and records what was granted or removed.

## Administrators (HR administrators)

- **Overview** shows what needs attention. **My tasks** lists steps assigned to you.
- **Start a workflow** from Onboarding, Offboarding, or Active workflows.
  - Choose **Test run** to practise. Test runs never send or change anything outside Baxter, and you can **Simulate approval** in Approvals.
  - Choose **Live** for real events.
- Open a workflow to complete steps. Each step type enforces its requirements: fields, checklist ticks, uploaded file, or completion note.
- **Approvals → Needs attention** lists anything that failed or needs to be done by hand. Use **Retry** after fixing a connection, or **Mark done by hand** with a note.
- Edit workflows in **Library & editor**. See [Configuration guides](configuration-guides.md).
- **Documents** lists every private document. Verify or reject uploads; downloads are logged.

## Employees

- **My tasks** shows steps assigned to you, such as uploading your signed paperwork. You can't see anyone else's records.
- You can upload documents only for yourself.
- Ask Baxter HR questions as usual. Baxter brings in Sam when needed, and Sam answers only from approved documents and your own information.
