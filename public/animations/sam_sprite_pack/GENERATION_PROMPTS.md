# Generation Prompt Set

These assets were created with the built-in image-generation workflow using `originals/reference/sam.png` as the identity reference.

## Shared specification

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset: transparent 2x2 web-animation sprite sheet
- Subject: Sam, the exact black-and-white voxel cat in the reference
- Preserve: tuxedo markings, bright green eyes, pink nose, voxel construction, proportions, blue bandana, and yellow A emblem
- Layout: equal square cells in row-major order, consistent camera/scale/lighting, complete figure and props within each cell, no dividers
- Constraints: genuinely transparent background; no readable text, extra characters, watermark, or cropped body parts

## Sheet-specific prompts

1. **Idle:** neutral stance; gentle blink; attentive HR-style look-around with tilted ear; welcoming tail flick and paw gesture.
2. **Walk right:** four natural right-facing gait phases with a friendly, quietly confident posture.
3. **Walk left:** four natural left-facing gait phases with subtly alert ear motion.
4. **Work:** HR desk routine alternating between laptop typing, personnel-folder review, checklist work, and typing; consistent desk and compact props.
5. **Sit down:** three continuous poses—begin lowering, halfway crouched, comfortably seated; fourth cell empty.
6. **Stand up:** three continuous poses—prepare to rise, halfway upright with subtle stretch, standing alert; fourth cell empty.
7. **People thinking:** attentive listening, kind reflective chin pose, reassuring people-first resolution with a simple heart-and-check symbol; fourth cell empty.
8. **HR task:** review a text-free employee-profile clipboard, offer a blue-and-gold welcome badge, organize a personnel folder with a friendly paw-up; fourth cell empty.
